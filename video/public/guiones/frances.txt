Seven routes lead to Santiago. Only one is called the French Way.
780 km from Saint Jean Pied de Port to Santiago. 33 stages.
Why is it THE one? It's the most walked. The best signposted. And there's a village, a bed and a café every few kilometres.
And here's what most people don't know: you don't have to walk all of it. The last stretch, Sarria to Santiago, is the part most pilgrims actually do.
Same route. Same Compostela. Your bag travels ahead, your room is waiting, and someone picks up the phone if you need it.
